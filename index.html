Help & Troubleshooting

Internet Support Guide

General guidance for common connectivity situations and technical assistance.

Slow Internet

Ensure your device is within range of the Wi-Fi router. Restarting your router can also clear temporary connection bottlenecks.

Connection Drops

Check all physical cable connections to your router and wall outlets to ensure they are securely fastened without damage.

Router Connectivity Issue

Verify that power indicators are active on your router. If lights are unresponsive, try power cycling the device.

Wi-Fi Coverage Problem

Position your router in a central open area away from thick concrete walls and electronic appliances that cause interference.

No Internet Connection

Check if all indicator lights on your modem/router are functioning normally. Contact our support team if the issue persists.

Installation & Configuration

Have questions about setting up a new router or connecting devices? Our technical support team is ready to assist you.
